Terms of sale

These terms apply to reading books online on this site. The paperback is ordered on Amazon, under its own terms.

The plans

  • Subscription, Can$15.99 a month: all the books, renewed every month until you cancel.
  • Online purchase, Can$20.00: the book, to read online on this site, with no time limit.
  • Rental, Can$4.99: the book, to read online for 7 days from payment.

Prices and tax

Prices in Canadian dollars. No tax is added to the price shown.

Payment

By card, on Stripe’s secure page. The site never sees your card number.

Cancelling the subscription

At any time, in My account, with “Cancel my subscription”. Access stays open until the end of the paid month; the month already started is not refunded.

Refunds

Full refund if a technical problem stops you from reading a book you paid for, or if you paid twice for the same thing: email us within 30 days of payment. Otherwise, since access is immediate, purchases and rentals are not refunded.

A refunded payment, or one disputed with your bank, removes the access it gave.

After payment

You receive a confirmation email: what you paid for, the price, the date, the order reference and a link to these terms.

Your rights

These terms do not take away any right you have under the Quebec Consumer Protection Act.
