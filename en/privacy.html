Privacy policy

What we do with your personal information on this site: what we collect, why, how long we keep it, who we share it with and how to exercise your rights. “We” means the publisher of the site, named in the legal notice. This policy follows Quebec’s law on the protection of personal information in the private sector (Law 25) and Canada’s federal law (PIPEDA); one part is for visitors from the European Union.

What we collect, and why

We collect only what the service you use needs. You give it to us yourself, or the site records it as you use it.

  • Your account: your email address, your language and, if you give them, your first and last name; the date you first signed in and the dates of your sessions. To sign you in and keep your account.
  • Signing in with Google, if you choose it: Google sends us your email address and an account ID, nothing else.
  • Reading online: the books you can read (subscription, purchase, rental, gift) and their dates; your place in each book and your bookmarks (book, chapter and its title, position in the text, dates). To open your books and let you pick up where you left off. No passage of the book is kept, nor your IP address.
  • Payment: for each order, the plan, the book, the amount, the tax, any refund, the date and the payment references at Stripe; the ID of your customer record at Stripe. Your card is entered at Stripe: the site never sees it.
  • The newsletter: your email address, your language, the page where you signed up, the date and version of the consent text, the dates you confirmed and unsubscribed. To send you the newsletter and be able to prove your consent.
  • A testimony: the book, your text, the highlighted sentence and video link if you give them, your first name, your city, your country if you give it, your email address, the language, the date and version of the consent text, and your account if you are signed in. To read it, publish it with your first name and city, and write to you about it. Your email address and country are never published.
  • A comment: your text and your account; your account’s first name is published with the comment (“A reader” if there is none). If you report a comment, your account is recorded with the report. To publish and moderate comments.
  • Emails: we write to you only about what you asked for (sign-in link, newsletter confirmation, testimony receipt, payment confirmation) and, if you signed up, the newsletter.
  • Security: to block abuse, the site briefly counts requests from the same IP address or for the same email address, in memory only, without recording anything. The server and Cloudflare also keep technical logs (IP address, page requested, date), for security and to keep the site running.

No advertising, no audience tracking, no profiling: the site does not follow you from site to site. Your browser’s time zone is used only to suggest the Amazon store of your country; it is neither kept nor sent.

Your reading place

When you read online, the site keeps your place in each book on your account, so you can pick up where you left off on any device. This setting, “Keep my place on my account”, is on from the moment the account is created.

To turn it off: My account, “My reading places” section, or “Reading settings” in the reading studio. Once it is off, your place stays only in your browser, and the places your account kept are erased. Your bookmarks, which you set yourself, stay until you remove them. “Erase my reading places and bookmarks”, in My account, erases everything without touching your account or your books.

This information can reveal your beliefs: we treat it as sensitive. No one on the site’s team can see it, it is shared with no one and is used only to let you pick up your reading. Only the host, who keeps the database, has technical access to it.

Without an account, your place in an excerpt stays in your browser, on this device, for 30 days at most.

Cookies and browser storage

A cookie is a small file the site places in your browser. This site uses only three, all needed for what you ask it to do; none is used for advertising or audience tracking. That is why the site does not ask you to accept cookies.

  • “payload-token”: keeps you signed in. 30 days, or until you sign out.
  • “connexion-google”: links Google’s reply to your sign-in request. 10 minutes.
  • “ticket-lecture”: opens the pages of the book you are reading. 10 minutes, renewed while you read.

The site also keeps, in your browser’s storage, information that never leaves your device:

  • “malu.studio.reglages”: the text size and background colour of the reading studio.
  • “malu.studio.position.<book>”: your place in a book, if you read without an account or turned off “Keep my place on my account”. 30 days.
  • “malu.studio.info-page”: remembers that you have seen the note about your reading place.

A video loads only when you click it: YouTube (Google) then receives your IP address and may set its own cookies. Stripe’s payment page and portal also set their own. You can erase all of this by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Who receives your information

The site’s team sees only what its role requires: for example, the person who moderates testimonies does not see your email address.

We hand some tasks to service providers, who may use your information only on our behalf:

  • the site’s host which keeps the site and its database;
  • Cloudflare (United States), which protects the site and speeds it up: every page goes through it;
  • Brevo (France), which sends the site’s emails and the newsletter;
  • Stripe (Canada and United States), which takes payments: we send it your email address, your reader ID, the book and plan chosen and the language of the page; Stripe itself collects your card, name, country and postal code;
  • Google (United States), if you choose “Continue with Google”, or when you start a YouTube video.

These providers handle your information outside Quebec, sometimes outside Canada, where the laws are different. Before sharing any with them, we check that it will be properly protected there, and we sign a written agreement with them that requires it.

We do not sell, rent or lend your information. We share it with no one else without your consent, except when the law requires or allows it, for example under a court order.

How long we keep it

  • Your account: as long as it exists. An account never confirmed (sign-in link never used) is deleted after 7 days.
  • The sign-in link: 15 minutes, and it works only once. The session: 30 days.
  • Your reading places and bookmarks: erased after 24 months without reading the book, when the book is deleted from the site, or with your account.
  • Orders: 6 years after the end of the year of purchase, as tax laws require. If you delete your account sooner, they stay, without your name.
  • The newsletter: as long as you are signed up. After you unsubscribe, we keep the address and the dates of the subscription, to prove your past consent and to stop writing to you; you can ask us to erase them. A confirmation link is valid for 7 days.
  • A testimony or comment: until you ask for it to be removed, or with your account.
  • Technical logs: a short time, as set by the host and Cloudflare.

Stripe, Brevo and Google keep their own data under their own policies and legal duties.

Your rights

At any time, you can:

  • find out what information we hold about you, and get a copy;
  • have it corrected if it is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date;
  • withdraw your consent: unsubscribe from the newsletter, turn off “Keep my place on my account”, ask for a testimony to be removed;
  • receive your information in a common format that another service can read;
  • ask us to stop publishing information about you, or to remove a link to it from search engines, in the cases the law provides;
  • delete your account and everything attached to it.

The easiest way: My account. There you can change your first name, last name, language and newsletter subscription. The “My data” section lists everything the site keeps about you; “Download my data” gives you a full copy (JSON file); “Delete my account” erases the account.

For anything else, write to the person in charge. We reply within 30 days, free of charge. We may ask you to prove your identity, so as not to give anything to someone else.

Security and incidents

Exchanges with the site are encrypted. There is no password to steal: sign-in links work only once and are not stored in readable form. The team’s access is limited to its role, and the site never sees your card. No system is foolproof, but we take reasonable measures and review them.

If an incident affects your information (access, loss or disclosure without permission) and presents a risk of serious harm, we notify you and the Commission d’accès à l’information, and we take steps to limit its effects. We keep a register of these incidents.

Minors

The site is meant for adults. A person under 14 must not create an account, sign up for the newsletter or send a testimony without the consent of a parent or guardian. If we learn that they did so without this consent, we erase their information; a parent can write to us for this.

Visitors from the European Union

If you are in the European Union, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) also applies:

  • the account, online reading, your reading place and payment serve to provide the service you ask for;
  • the newsletter and testimonies rest on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time;
  • orders are kept to meet our tax obligations;
  • technical logs and abuse blocking protect the site.

You can also object to processing or ask for it to be restricted. Your information is processed in Canada, which the European Union recognises as offering adequate protection, and by providers in the United States, covered by the safeguards the GDPR provides. You can lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country.

Complaints

You can write to us first: we will look for a solution with you. You can also contact the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Changes

If we change this policy, the new version appears here, with its date. An important change is announced to you by email before it applies. We do not use your information for a new purpose without your consent, unless the law allows it.
