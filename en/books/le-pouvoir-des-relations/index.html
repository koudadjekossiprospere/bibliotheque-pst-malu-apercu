Le Pouvoir des Relations

Our relationships are invisible forces that sculpt our walk. Some uplift, enlighten and propel. Others hold back, lead astray or wound. Every bond carries a power: the power to open or close paths in our destiny.

Through a deep, biblical and inward reading, this book unveils the influences that build up or tear down, those to avoid, those to discern and those to protect. It reveals how relational dynamics, visible or hidden, steer our choices, our identity and our future.

An essential guide to understanding the bonds that shape destiny, healing what has been broken, recognizing what must be released and walking in relationships that are aligned, healthy and life-giving.

The author

Malu Bialuta

“A geologist by training, an entrepreneur at heart.”

I am a pastor at the Impact Centre Chrétien church in Montreal, a trainer, teacher and missionary. I have preached in several countries on transformation, emotional healing and the restoration of the soul, and my ministry focuses on training spiritual leaders.

A musician and songwriter, I have led youth groups and worship teams, and founded the gospel group HOWzik. A geologist by training and an entrepreneur at heart, I am married to Sandrine and the father of three children.
