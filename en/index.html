The author

Malu Bialuta

“A geologist by training, an entrepreneur at heart.”

Author’s biography, back cover

I am a pastor at the Impact Centre Chrétien church in Montreal, a trainer, teacher and missionary. I have preached in several countries on transformation, emotional healing and the restoration of the soul, and my ministry focuses on training spiritual leaders.

A musician and songwriter, I have led youth groups and worship teams, and founded the gospel group HOWzik. A geologist by training and an entrepreneur at heart, I am married to Sandrine and the father of three children.
